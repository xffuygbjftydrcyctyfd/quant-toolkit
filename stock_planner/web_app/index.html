Before you see your projection

  • This is a financial education tool, not personalized investment advice. The holdings shown are what a mean-variance model selects from a fixed universe under stated assumptions — not a recommendation to buy any security. It doesn't know your taxes, debts, or personal circumstances.
  • Return estimates are the weakest link. They come from recent history, heavily shrunk toward sector averages because raw historical means are unreliable — and they will still be wrong to some degree. The weight ranges shown next to each holding exist to make that uncertainty visible.
  • The probability is a model output, not a promise. A 70% chance of reaching your goal is also a 3-in-10 chance of falling short.
  • For decisions involving real money, consult a licensed financial advisor.

Assumptions & methodology

Data. Annualized return and covariance estimates from ~5 years of daily history. Covariance uses Ledoit-Wolf shrinkage (raw sample covariance is too noisy to invert reliably). Expected returns are a disclosed blend: 50% Black-Litterman equilibrium (the returns implied by market-cap weights being optimal), 30% sector-shrunk historical means, 20% momentum-tilted (12-1 month, capped at ±2%/yr) — because raw historical means are the least reliable input in portfolio optimization. Factor exposures are regression betas on universe-constructed MKT/SMB/HML factors. With very large universes (S&P 500 mode), optimization runs on fixed income plus the top 150 equities by return/volatility ratio after your filters.

Optimization. Long-only mean-variance: maximize w'μ − (λ/2)·w'Σw, fully invested, per-stock cap 15% and per-equity-sector cap 35% (both loosen proportionally if you narrow the universe below three sectors; fixed income is uncapped). Solved by accelerated projected gradient (FISTA) with exact capped-simplex projection and sector-cap penalty continuation — validated against an independent SciPy implementation to objective differences below 1e-4.

Risk score. Questionnaire (0–18 raw) scales to 0–100, adjusted by horizon: under 3 years caps at 25, under 7 caps at 60, over 15 adds 8. Score maps to λ = 10^(2 − 2.3·score/100).

Goal simulation. 6,000 Monte Carlo paths of monthly lognormal returns at the portfolio's blended return/volatility (assumes continuous rebalancing), monthly contributions added, final values compared against your target grown at 2.5%/yr inflation.

Not modeled: taxes, trading costs, dividends vs. price-return distinctions, currency, liquidity, and your personal circumstances. Not investment advice.
