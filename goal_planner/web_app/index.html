Before you see your projection

  • This is a financial education tool, not personalized investment advice. It doesn't know your taxes, debts, health, family situation, or anything else a real advisor would ask about.
  • The projections are built on assumptions, not knowledge of the future. Expected returns for each asset class are long-run estimates — reasonable, stated openly below the results, and certainly wrong to some degree.
  • The probability you'll see is a model output, not a promise. A 75% chance of reaching your goal also means a 1-in-4 chance of falling short.
  • For decisions involving real money, consult a licensed financial advisor.

Assumptions & methodology

Risk questionnaire (0–18 raw) is scaled to 0–100, then adjusted by horizon: under 3 years caps the score at 25, under 7 years caps it at 60, over 15 years adds 8 (long horizons can carry more risk). The score maps to a risk-aversion parameter λ = 10^(2 − 2.3·score/100), and the allocation maximizes w'μ − (λ/2)·w'Σw, long-only, fully invested.

The goal simulation runs 6,000 Monte Carlo paths of monthly lognormal returns at the portfolio's blended return/volatility (equivalent to continuous rebalancing), adds your monthly contribution each month, and compares final values against your target grown at 2.5%/yr inflation. Every formula here was validated against an independent Python/SciPy implementation.

Not investment advice. No taxes, fees, or personal circumstances are modeled.
